OUR STORY

A little taste of Italy in every dish.

Casa Milano was created from a simple idea: bring the warmth, flavors and traditions of Italy to every table.

From our handmade pasta to our wood-fired pizzas, everything is prepared with carefully selected ingredients and recipes inspired by generations of Italian cooking.
